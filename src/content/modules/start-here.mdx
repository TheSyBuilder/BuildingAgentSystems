<section className="taxonomy-section" aria-labelledby="taxonomy-title">
  <div className="section-intro section-intro-inverse">
    <div>
      <p className="module-kicker">Five control patterns</p>
      <h2 id="taxonomy-title">Name the operating mode.</h2>
    </div>
    <p>
      These are architecture lenses, not mutually exclusive product categories.
      One product can combine several; inspect each part by who owns its path.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div className="taxonomy-grid">
    <article className="taxonomy-card">
      <span className="card-index">01</span>
      <p className="control-owner">The user owns each turn</p>
      <h3>Chatbot</h3>
      <p>
        A conversational surface that returns a response. The exchange can be
        useful and sophisticated, but the system does not own completion of a
        multi-step job.
      </p>
      <p className="card-test"><strong>Test:</strong> the work ends when the answer arrives.</p>
    </article>

    <article className="taxonomy-card">
      <span className="card-index">02</span>
      <p className="control-owner">A trigger owns the start</p>
      <h3>Automation</h3>
      <p>
        A known event runs a fixed rule or action: assign a default label, send
        a reminder, copy a record. The route is encoded before execution.
      </p>
      <p className="card-test"><strong>Test:</strong> same trigger, same programmed action.</p>
    </article>

    <article className="taxonomy-card">
      <span className="card-index">03</span>
      <p className="control-owner">Code owns the route</p>
      <h3>Deterministic workflow</h3>
      <p>
        Software coordinates several known steps and branches. A model may
        transform or classify inside a step, while code still chooses what runs
        next.
      </p>
      <p className="card-test"><strong>Test:</strong> the path can be drawn before the run.</p>
    </article>

    <article className="taxonomy-card">
      <span className="card-index">04</span>
      <p className="control-owner">The human owns the route</p>
      <h3>Copilot</h3>
      <p>
        A person stays at the controls while the model drafts, explains,
        suggests, or prepares a move in the context of their work.
      </p>
      <p className="card-test"><strong>Test:</strong> the person chooses every next move.</p>
    </article>

    <article className="taxonomy-card taxonomy-card-agent">
      <span className="card-index">05</span>
      <p className="control-owner">The model owns the next-step choice</p>
      <h3>Agent</h3>
      <p>
        Within explicit guardrails, a model uses current evidence to choose the
        next step or tool, checks progress, and continues until a stop condition
        or human handoff.
      </p>
      <p className="card-test"><strong>Test:</strong> the exact route emerges during the run.</p>
    </article>
  </div>

  <aside className="overlap-note" aria-label="How the patterns overlap">
    <strong>Interfaces are not architectures.</strong>
    <p>
      A chatbot can front an agent. A copilot can launch an automation. An agent
      can call a deterministic workflow. Label each boundary by who controls it.
    </p>
  </aside>
</section>

  <ol className="decision-list">
    <li>
      <span className="decision-index" aria-hidden="true">01</span>
      <div className="decision-question">
        <h3>Does the job end when a response is delivered?</h3>
        <p>There is no owned multi-step outcome and no action after the answer.</p>
      </div>
      <div className="decision-branches">
        <p><span>Yes</span><strong>Use a chatbot.</strong></p>
        <p><span>No</span><strong>Continue to 02.</strong></p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span className="decision-index" aria-hidden="true">02</span>
      <div className="decision-question">
        <h3>Can the correct path and its branches be specified in advance?</h3>
        <p>The exceptions are known, testable, and cheaper to encode than interpret.</p>
      </div>
      <div className="decision-branches">
        <p><span>Yes</span><strong>Use automation for one action; a deterministic workflow for several.</strong></p>
        <p><span>No</span><strong>Continue to 03.</strong></p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span className="decision-index" aria-hidden="true">03</span>
      <div className="decision-question">
        <h3>Will a person remain available to choose every next move?</h3>
        <p>The model can prepare work, but the person keeps sequence and judgment.</p>
      </div>
      <div className="decision-branches">
        <p><span>Yes</span><strong>Use a copilot.</strong></p>
        <p><span>No</span><strong>Continue to 04.</strong></p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span className="decision-index" aria-hidden="true">04</span>
      <div className="decision-question">
        <h3>Can independent steps be bounded, observed, verified, and stopped?</h3>
        <p>Name the tools, permissions, evidence, budgets, stop conditions, and handoff.</p>
      </div>
      <div className="decision-branches">
        <p><span>Yes</span><strong>You have an agent candidate.</strong></p>
        <p><span>No</span><strong>Keep it human-led or shrink the job until those controls exist.</strong></p>
      </div>
    </li>
  </ol>
</section>
